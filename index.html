Property Entry / Labour Waiver

If labour requires entering a property, stairs, elevators, hallways, driveways, garages, yards, or other access areas, customer confirms authority to allow access. HiveHaul™ Service Terms
1
Service Scope
HiveHaul™ provides curb-to-curb transport services unless additional labour is requested and confirmed. If labour is requested, it may include lifting, loading, unloading, and placement of items as reasonably agreed before the job begins.
2
Client Responsibilities
The client agrees to provide accurate details about item size, weight, pickup/drop-off access, stairs, elevators, parking/loading access, and any tight turns or limited access. Items must be safely prepared, secured, wrapped where needed, and ready for transport at the scheduled time.
3
Access, Safety & Job Conditions
Additional time, effort, helpers, or charges may apply if job conditions differ from the information provided. HiveHaul™ may refuse unsafe, impractical, illegal, oversized, or high-risk work. This includes unsafe lifting, restricted access, difficult stairs, narrow spaces, or conditions that may cause damage or injury.
4
Labour & Property Entry
HiveHaul™ standard service is curb-to-curb. If labour requires entering a property, building, elevator, hallway, garage, yard, driveway, or other access area, the client confirms they have authority to allow access. Labour add-ons may be billed hourly and may increase the final quote based on time, weight, access, and safety.
5
No Cabin Access
No passengers, pets, or customer items are permitted inside the vehicle cab at any time. Only the HiveHaul™ driver/operator may occupy the cabin. No exceptions.
6
Legal Cargo Only
The client confirms all cargo is legal under Canadian federal and provincial law. No illegal drugs, controlled substances, firearms, stolen goods, hazardous materials, or prohibited items are permitted. HiveHaul™ may refuse or terminate service if cargo appears unsafe, prohibited, or unlawful.
7
Pricing, Deposit & Payment
Quotes are based on the information provided at the time of request. Final pricing may be adjusted if job conditions differ materially from what was submitted. A deposit may be required to secure the booking. Remaining balance is due upon completion and before offloading, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Payment accepted by E-Transfer to hivehaulca@gmail.com or cash. Booking is confirmed only once the required deposit is received.
8
Photos, Item Condition & Documentation
Clients are encouraged to document item condition before pickup. HiveHaul™ may capture photos at pickup and delivery for record-keeping, safety, and dispute prevention. Pre-existing damage, wear, weak structure, loose parts, or poor packing is not the responsibility of HiveHaul™.
9
Transport Risk & Liability
HiveHaul™ takes reasonable care when handling and transporting items. Transporting heavy, used, fragile, oversized, or poorly packed items involves inherent risk. HiveHaul™ does not provide full-value cargo insurance. Any claim must be supported by clear photos and job records from before pickup and after delivery.
10
Cancellations & Changes
Clients should provide reasonable notice for cancellations or changes. Deposits may be non-refundable depending on timing, scheduling impact, and whether the team has already been assigned or dispatched.
